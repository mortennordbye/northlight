{{- /* tabs — tabbed panels. **The markup is not tabs.** It is a plain sequence of headed
elements, which is what a reader gets with JavaScript disabled: every panel visible, one after another, each under its own heading. That is a complete, readable document rather than a degraded one. `assets/js/tabs.js` upgrades it in place to a real tablist — role="tablist", role="tab", role="tabpanel", aria-selected, aria-controls and arrow-key navigation. Building it the other way round, as a tablist that JavaScript makes usable, is the version that leaves a reader with no script staring at a pile of unlabelled divs. Headings are h3, matching `list`: a page's own sections are h2, so a panel heading nests under them rather than reading as ending the section it sits in. Parameters: group optional name; tabs sharing a group switch together across a page Call: {{< tabs >}}{{< tab label="One" >}}…{{< /tab >}}{{< /tabs >}} */ -}}
