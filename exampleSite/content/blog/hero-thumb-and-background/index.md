This post exists to render `heroStyle = "thumbAndBackground"`. Every other post on this site uses the
default, `basic`.

**Its cover has no text in it, and that is the point.** The covers elsewhere on this site
have their title baked into the artwork, which is the right choice for a card but the
wrong one here: an overlay treatment would then show the title twice. Artwork for these
styles should carry no words.

The image is still never cropped. The box keeps the exact 1200×630 ratio and the fit is
`contain`, so the whole cover is visible and the header sits over a scrim on top of it.
